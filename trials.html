TRIALS & REGISTRATION

Cricket ka asli chance
starts at the trial.

Selection into JCC isn't first-come-first-served — every player goes through an open talent trial before joining a season.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from trial to final

01

Attend an open trial

Trials are held at grounds across Delhi and Noida. Players are assessed on batting, bowling, fielding, speed and agility — not just cricket technique.

02

Get shortlisted

Coaches shortlist players by age division based on trial performance. Shortlisting is purely on the day's assessment.

03

Player auction

Shortlisted players go into a season auction and are drafted onto club teams — mirroring how a professional league forms its squads.

04

League to finals

Teams play a coached, structured league that builds toward semi-finals and a final at a marquee ground.

Before you go

Carry your own kit if you have one, wear sports shoes, and arrive early — trials assess fitness and agility alongside cricket skill.

On payments

JCC follows a no-cash policy. Any registration or season fee is collected digitally — UPI, card or bank transfer — never cash in hand.

Trial dates open on Instagram first

JCC posts every trial date, ground and time on Instagram before it's announced anywhere else. DM to confirm your slot.
