WHAT JCC DOES

Built like a real league, sized for young players

01

Open Talent Trials

Trials are held across Delhi-NCR grounds and test batting, bowling, fielding, speed and agility — not just who you know.

02

Player Auctions

Selected players go into a season auction, drafted onto club teams — the same format young fans watch on TV.

03

Official Kits

Every squad plays in official team jerseys, giving matches a professional feel from the very first over.

04

Coached Matches

League games are structured and coached, so every match is also a learning session, not just a fixture.

05

Age-Wise Divisions

Players compete within their own age bracket, roughly spanning Under-14 through broader 8–18 divisions.

06

Finals at Marquee Grounds

Each season builds toward semi-finals and finals at a larger ground, giving players a real big-match feeling.

TRANSPARENCY

What JCC does — and doesn't do

JCC DOES

  • Run open, trial-based selection for every player
  • Organise age-appropriate divisions, roughly 8–18
  • Provide official jerseys to selected squads
  • Coach and structure every league match
  • Host finals at bigger, marquee grounds

JCC DOES NOT

  • Guarantee selection without attending trials
  • Accept cash payments — digital/UPI only
  • Promise professional or BCCI-level contracts
  • Process registrations outside official channels
  • Share player data with unrelated third parties
