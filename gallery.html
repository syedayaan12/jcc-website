Season 8, wrapped.

A look back at the most recently completed season — from open trials to the final at OP Sports Ground & Farm, Noida.

SEASON 8 TIMELINE

How the season played out

STAGE 01

Open Trials

Talent trials held across Delhi and Noida, including grounds like the Jamia Millia Islamia Sports Complex.

STAGE 02

Player Auction

Shortlisted players were drafted into club teams during the season auction.

STAGE 03

League Matches

Coached, structured league matches were played across the season between club teams.

STAGE 04 — FINALS

Season 8 Final

Season 8 concluded in July 2026 at OP Sports Ground & Farm, Noida, featuring club teams including VVIP Little Champs and Noida Tigers.
