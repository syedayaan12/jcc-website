ABOUT JCC

A professional-style platform,
built for young players.

JCC gives players in Delhi-NCR the structure of a real league — trials, auctions, official jerseys and coached matches — at an age where most cricket is still played on borrowed grounds.

OUR STORY

Grassroots cricket,
run like a league.

Junior Cricket Championship (JCC) organises competitive, trial-based cricket for young players across the Delhi-NCR region. Instead of casual matches, JCC runs seasons — open talent trials, a player auction that drafts squads, official team jerseys, and a coached league that builds toward semi-finals and finals at a bigger ground.

The idea is simple: give young players from any neighbourhood a fair shot at being seen, coached, and competing at a level closer to what professional cricket looks like — just scaled for their age.
